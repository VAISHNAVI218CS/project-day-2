ABOUT THE STATE

Karnataka at a glance

Karnataka is a state where ancient history and modern life exist side by side. Located in southern India, it is known for its architectural heritage, natural landscapes, technology hubs and diverse culture.

From the ruins of Hampi and the royal streets of Mysuru to the coffee plantations of Coorg and the beaches of coastal Karnataka, every region offers something different.

Karnataka brings together heritage, wildlife, spirituality, food, technology and natural beauty, making it one of India's diverse travel destinations.
